Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026. This policy covers the Starbound Duo Android app and its game server.

In short: you can play without an account. If you sign in with Google, the game stores your game progress, scores and a display name so you can keep your progress and appear on the leaderboards. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Playing without an account

Solo games and same-room duo games work without signing in. Your progress, scores and settings are then kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and never sent to us.

What we store when you sign in

DataWhy
Your Google account ID, and your email address if Google reports it as verified To recognise your account when you sign in again. We never see your Google password.
A display name (at first, the name on your Google account) Shown to other players on leaderboards and in duo parties. You can change it any time in the app (Account → Rename).
Game progress: best scores, highest sector, totals (games, snacks, enemies), unlocks and settings Your cloud save, so your progress follows you to another phone.
Scores with their date, game length and, for duo games, your partner's player number Leaderboards, and checking that duo scores were earned together.
Sign-in sessions (stored only as one-way hashes) and the time of your last sign-in To keep you signed in securely.

Online duo parties (the 6-character codes) and who you played with are kept for at most a few hours. The moves sent between two phones during a game are passed straight through the server and are not stored.

What other players can see

Your display name and your best scores on the leaderboards, and your display name to the partner in a duo party. Nothing else, not your email address or Google account.

Same-room duo (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi)

To find the other phone, Android requires the Nearby devices permission and, on some versions, Location. The game uses them only to connect the two phones. It does not record or send your location. The other phone sees your phone's model name (for example "Pixel 8") in its list.

On your phone

The app keeps your progress, settings and the server address in its private storage. If you sign in, it also keeps a session token, encrypted with a key held by Android's secure keystore. Uninstalling the app removes all of this from the phone.

Other companies involved

  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" on your phone, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare carries the connection between the app and the game server, and so processes your IP address while you are connected (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • GitHub hosts this website and the small file the app reads to find the game server's current address, so it sees your IP address when the app checks it (GitHub's privacy statement).

We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell or rent your data.

Where it is stored, and for how long

The game server is run by the developer. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Weekly leaderboards reset every week; all-time leaderboard entries stay until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, cloud save and scores from the server; your entries disappear from the leaderboards. Progress stored on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. You can also ask for deletion by email (below).

Security

The app talks to the server only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in sessions are stored as one-way hashes, and the session on your phone is encrypted.

Children

Starbound Duo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
