StarboundDuo

Blobbits are raiding Earth's snack supply. Fly together and take it back.

Android · in testing

Starbound Duo is a cartoon space shooter built for two. Each player flies their own ship on their own phone: shoot down waves of Blobbits, grab snacks and weapon gifts, and beat the Mega Blobbit boss together. You can also play alone.

  • Two phones, one ship eachPlay side by side over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, no internet needed.
  • Or play from anywhereCreate an online party and share a 6-character code with your partner.
  • Seven weaponsSpread, Laser, Homing, Vulcan, Lightning, Plasma and Ripple, each up to level 5.
  • Teamwork paysFly close for a fused attack, charge a Duo Ultimate, revive a fallen partner.
  • LeaderboardsWeekly and all-time boards for solo players and for duo pairs.
  • Cloud saveSign in with Google to keep your progress across phones.
